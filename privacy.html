Privacy Policy

Your client work should stay clear and controlled.

This policy explains how FollowUp Copilot handles client messages, screenshots, reminders, subscriptions, and AI analysis. Last updated: May 21, 2026.

Information the app handles

Client leads and messages

Client leads, pasted messages, generated replies, stages, and reminder times are stored locally on your device.

AI message analysis

When you choose to analyze a message, the message text and selected business type are sent to the app's secure assistant service to generate a summary, intent, urgency, and reply drafts.

Screenshots

Selected screenshots are processed on device using Apple's Vision framework to extract text. You can edit extracted text before sending it for AI analysis.

Notifications

Follow-up reminders use local notifications. You can disable notifications in iOS Settings at any time.

Subscriptions

Purchases and subscription status are handled by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. The app does not receive your full payment card details.

How information is used

Provide app features

Information is used to organize leads, generate reply drafts, schedule reminders, and restore subscription access.

Improve reliability

Support requests may be used to diagnose issues. Please avoid sending private client information in support reports.

No sale of personal information

FollowUp Copilot does not sell personal information.

Data retention and control

Local lead data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. AI analysis requests are processed to provide the requested result. You control what message text is submitted for analysis.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, open a request through GitHub Issues.
